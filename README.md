## About Me

I am a self-taught developer and full-time dad, originally from Fiji and now based in Western Sydney, Australia. With over 7 years of diverse experience across caregiving, customs brokerage, logistics, retail, and commercial operations, I bring a pragmatic, real-world perspective to software engineering and AI automation.

- **Founder** of [ClaudeKing.org](https://claudeking.org) (AI automation agency) and Clean Up Bros (commercial cleaning).
- **Co-founder** of BulaRides (Fiji ride-hailing).
- **Creator of Koda** — a 24/7 AI operator running autonomously on a Mac Mini in Sydney.
- **Languages:** English, Fijian, Hindi.
